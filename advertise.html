Advertise with GlobalMoneyCalc

Connect your brand with people using practical tools for currency conversion, salary planning, monthly budgets and savings estimates—in English and Bengali.

Why partner with this project?

  • An international, bilingual website with English and Bengali interface options.
  • Practical calculator categories connected to everyday money questions.
  • A simple browser-based experience; visitors do not need to create an account.

Audience size, geography, impressions and traffic statistics are not published yet. We will not invent numbers or promise campaign results.

Partnership options

  • Display sponsorship: a clearly marked brand placement in an available advertising area, subject to site readiness and policy requirements.
  • Calculator sponsorship: a labelled “sponsored by” acknowledgement near a relevant calculator, without changing its calculations.
  • Educational collaboration: factual, clearly labelled financial-literacy content reviewed before publication.

Our standards

  • Sponsored placements will be identified clearly.
  • Sponsors cannot buy changes to calculation results or independent tool descriptions.
  • We will not publish misleading “guaranteed profit” claims or unsupported financial promises.
  • We do not sell the amounts visitors enter into the calculators.
