Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

GlobalMoneyCalc provides browser-based calculators for general information.

Calculator data

Values entered into the calculators are processed in your browser. GlobalMoneyCalc does not send the amounts you enter into its calculators to a GlobalMoneyCalc database or to the exchange-rate provider. If you choose to save a calculation, its inputs and displayed result are stored only in local storage in that browser on that device; they are not uploaded to our servers and do not sync across devices. Clearing browser storage may erase saved calculations.

Optional account data

If you create an account and choose to use the private dashboard, the profile details you provide and the income/expense, debt and budget records you enter are sent to Supabase and stored with your account. Row Level Security policies restrict access to each account’s own records. Supabase Auth handles your password; GlobalMoneyCalc does not receive or store your plain-text password. Please review Supabase Privacy Policy.

Exchange-rate service and local cache

The homepage converter and currency-rate chart request indicative exchange-rate data from ExchangeRate.dev. The homepage requests a USD-based dataset; the chart requests rates for the base currency you select. These requests do not include the amount you enter into a calculator. Your browser performs the currency conversion locally. Rate responses may be cached in your browser for up to 15 minutes. ExchangeRate.dev may process standard technical request information under its own privacy terms. Please review ExchangeRate.dev Terms.

Local date and time

The clock at the very top of each page uses your device’s local clock and browser time-zone setting. This information is read locally in your browser and is not sent to GlobalMoneyCalc or the exchange-rate provider.

Hosting

This website is hosted using Cloudflare Pages. The hosting provider may process technical information such as request logs, IP addresses and browser details to operate and secure the service. Please review Cloudflare’s privacy policy for details.

Analytics, cookies and advertising

Google Analytics 4 is configured for this site. The Google Analytics tag loads only after a visitor selects “Allow analytics”; choosing “Reject” prevents the tag from loading. If accepted, Google may process usage and device/browser information to provide traffic reports. Calculator amounts are not sent to Google Analytics. The current site has no advertising network scripts. Exchange-rate data stored in browser local storage is not an advertising cookie. Review Google’s Privacy Policy for details.

External links

Third-party websites have their own privacy practices and policies. Review those policies before using external services.

Contact

For privacy questions, email globalmoneycalc@gmail.com. Do not send passwords, bank details or other sensitive information.
