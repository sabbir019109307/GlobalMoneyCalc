Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

GlobalMoneyCalc provides browser-based calculators for general information.

Calculator data

Values entered into the calculators are processed in your browser. GlobalMoneyCalc does not send the amounts you enter into its calculators to a GlobalMoneyCalc database or to the exchange-rate provider.

Exchange-rate service and local cache

The currency converter can request daily indicative exchange-rate data directly from ExchangeRate-API at open.er-api.com. The request asks for reference rates and does not include the amount you enter or the currency pair you calculate. Your browser performs the conversion locally. The returned reference-rate data may be stored in your browser local storage until the provider’s next scheduled update. You can clear this browser data through your browser settings. ExchangeRate-API may process standard technical request information under its own privacy terms. Please review ExchangeRate-API terms.

Hosting

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical information such as request logs, IP addresses and browser details to operate and secure the service. Please review GitHub’s privacy statement for details.

Cookies and advertising

The current version does not intentionally set advertising cookies or include third-party advertising scripts. The exchange-rate response is cached in browser local storage; this is not an advertising cookie. If analytics or advertising services are added later, this policy will be updated to describe the services, cookies and available choices before they are enabled.

External links

Third-party websites have their own privacy practices and policies. Review those policies before using external services.

Contact

For now, raise privacy questions through the project GitHub issue page. GitHub issues are public. Do not include passwords, account numbers or other sensitive information.
