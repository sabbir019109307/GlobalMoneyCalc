1. How to convert currencies

Choose the currency you are converting from and the currency you want to receive. Enter the amount. The automatic feed shows indicative market rates delayed by 15 minutes, or you can enter the exact rate quoted by your bank or exchange provider.

Converted amount = original amount × conversion rate

Before you exchange money

  • Free-feed rates are indicative and delayed by 15 minutes; they are not live cash-exchange or remittance quotes.
  • Banks and exchange services may apply their own buy/sell spread, transfer fee or minimum charge.
  • For a real transaction, compare the final amount you will receive after all fees.

The automatic converter calculates the result in your browser. It does not send the amount you entered to the exchange-rate provider.

2. How the salary calculator works

Enter gross monthly pay, the number of working days in a typical month, hours worked per day and any deduction percentage you want to estimate.

Net pay = monthly pay − (monthly pay × deduction percentage ÷ 100)

The daily estimate divides net pay by working days. The hourly estimate divides that daily figure by working hours per day. Choose the currency in which you entered your salary.

This is a planning estimate, not a payroll statement. Actual take-home pay can also depend on tax, overtime, allowances, leave, employer policy and local law.

3. How to plan a monthly budget

Enter your total monthly income and add the main expense groups: housing, food, transport and other spending. Use the same currency for every amount.

Money remaining = monthly income − total monthly expenses

A positive result is the amount left after the expenses entered. A negative result means the listed expenses are greater than the income entered. Review the categories regularly because household costs can change.

4. How the savings projection works

Enter the amount you expect to save each month, the number of months and an illustrative annual return percentage. Select the currency used for the monthly contribution.

Monthly rate = annual rate ÷ 12

The projection assumes the same contribution is made at the end of each month and compounds the stated rate monthly. With a zero rate, the result is simply the total of the contributions.

Actual savings products may pay different rates, apply tax or fees, and change their terms. Returns are never guaranteed by this calculator. This is not investment advice.
