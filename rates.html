Exchange-rate source and calculation method

Understand where the reference rates come from, how the converter calculates a currency pair and what the displayed numbers can—and cannot—tell you.

1. Where the rates come from

The automatic converter requests indicative market exchange rates from ExchangeRate.dev. The free/keyless feed is delayed by 15 minutes and includes source, market-session and observation timestamps. It is indicative data, not a bank or cash-exchange quote.

2. How a conversion is calculated

The data feed uses USD as its base currency. The converter compares the USD rates for the selected source and target currencies, then calculates the result in your browser.

Pair rate = USD reference rate for target ÷ USD reference rate for source
Converted amount = entered amount × pair rate

For example, the KWD-to-BDT rate is calculated by dividing the USD-to-BDT reference rate by the USD-to-KWD reference rate. The converter then multiplies your entered KWD amount by that pair rate.

3. Updates and browser cache

The converter reads the provider's observation timestamp and caches the downloaded rates in your browser for 15 minutes to reduce requests. The free/keyless feed itself is delayed by 15 minutes. If a refresh fails, a saved rate may be shown as stale. When a cached response is past its next-update time and a fresh request fails, the website labels it as stale so you can switch to a rate supplied by your bank or exchange provider.

You can remove the saved rate data by clearing this website's local storage or site data in your browser settings.

4. Important limitations

  • These are indicative market rates delayed by 15 minutes on the free/keyless feed, not live trading prices, bank transfer quotes or cash-exchange buy/sell rates.
  • The converter does not include a bank's spread, transfer fee, cash-exchange margin, card fee or other provider charges.
  • The provider describes its data as indicative and not for settlement or regulated trading. Check the final amount with your provider before a transaction.
  • The converter offers a selected list of popular currencies. Availability can change if the data provider changes its supported rates.

5. What information is sent?

The browser requests the shared USD-based reference-rate dataset. It does not include the amount you typed or the currency pair you selected. The multiplication and currency-pair calculation happen on your device. The API provider may still receive ordinary technical request information, such as the request's network address and browser-related metadata, under its own privacy practices.

Read the GlobalMoneyCalc Privacy Policy.

6. Use of provider data

The provider permits caching for your own end-use and use in currency-conversion tools, including commercial use, subject to its terms. Its terms prohibit redistribution of the raw rate data. GlobalMoneyCalc therefore presents calculated conversion results and does not offer a downloadable raw-rate feed. Review the provider's current documentation and terms before relying on the service.
