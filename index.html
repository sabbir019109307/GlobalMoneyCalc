Frequently asked questions

Understand the estimates before you use them.

Are exchange rates live?

The automatic converter uses indicative reference rates that are refreshed daily, not live bank or cash-exchange quotes. Fees and provider spreads are not included.

How is a currency conversion calculated?

The tool compares the two currencies against a common reference base and calculates the requested amount in your browser. You can also enter the exact rate your provider offers.

Are my calculator entries uploaded?

Your entered amounts are calculated in your browser and are not sent to GlobalMoneyCalc. The exchange-rate response may be cached in your browser for up to 15 minutes.

Can I use these results as financial advice?

No. Results are estimates to help with everyday planning. Confirm important amounts with your bank, employer or a qualified professional.
